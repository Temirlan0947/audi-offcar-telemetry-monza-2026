Lap quality

Distance, data gaps and timing precision

Distance is estimated by integrating speed over the original timestamps. A continuous lap is scaled once to the 5,793 m centreline. A gap longer than 1.0 s or a missing speed splits the trace. Each surviving piece needs a timing-line anchor. Hatched regions have no reliable distance comparison.

OpenF1 lap starts are approximate. The timing checks compare distance-based estimates against cumulative sector times. A line used as an anchor is marked in the table. Pedal readings use the preceding valid sample within each piece. Unknown values appear as a dash. Vmax uses recorded speed samples. Speed trap is the reading at a fixed timing point.

The public channels show changes in speed at full throttle. Battery charge and MGU-K power are unavailable in this feed.
